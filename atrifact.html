Osmotic Power Generation


Intended Purpose of this Technology:

The intended purpose of this technology is to be a clean source of power generation via the mixing of saline water and fresh water through a membrane


Work Cited “Production of Electric Power by mixing Fresh and Salt Water in the Hydroelectric Pile.” Production of Electric Power by mixing Fresh and Salt Water in the Hydroelectric Pile, 1954, https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/1954Natur.174..660P/abstract. Accessed 15 5 2026.
