Osmotic Power Generation


Intended Purpose of this Technology:

The intended purpose of this technology is to be a clean source of power generation via the mixing of saline water and fresh water through a membrane


The concept of this technology was first thought of by english physicist Richard Pattle (“Production of Electric Power by mixing Fresh and Salt Water in the Hydroelectric Pile”). The first known example of someone seeing this phenomenon in action was in 1973, when american chemical engineer Sidney Loeb, ther person who made the first practical methods of generating electricity out of his discovery, was doing research at where the jordan river and the dead sea meet. He realized that the massive chemical difference could be used for power generation, leading him to make the first practacle form of osmotic power generation.
